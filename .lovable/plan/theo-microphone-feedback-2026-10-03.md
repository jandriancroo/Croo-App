- Add a compact live voice meter directly below Theo’s orb while the microphone is listening.
- Drive it from the manager’s microphone input, not Theo’s playback, so movement confirms their voice is reaching the app.
- Keep it visible and calm when the room is quiet, with stronger movement as speech gets louder.

- Change only the Theo voice screen presentation and microphone-level display.
- Do not change voice timing, audio, cues, models, actions, permissions, or any locked feature.
- Use the existing theme colors and preserve the compact action/list layouts.
